01 / The Problem

Paper doesn't scale — and it definitely doesn't audit well.

Most inspection and QA/QC data in upstream oil & gas still lives in binders, spreadsheets and disconnected PDFs. That works fine until an audit, a client inspection, or a personnel handover forces you to reconstruct it fast.

Records live on paper

Weld reports, corrosion logs and PTW forms get filled out in the field and never make it into a searchable system.

No single source of truth

Personnel qualifications, inspection history and compliance status live in separate spreadsheets that fall out of sync.

Audits take days, not minutes

Assembling a compliance package for a client or regulator means manually hunting down binders across departments.

Certifications lapse quietly

Without a live register, it's easy to miss when an inspector's NDT qualification is about to expire.

07 / FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

If your question isn't here, ask it directly on the demo call.

Is VetriX•IQ built around a specific inspection standard?

The Qualification module is built on ASME Section IX logic, and the platform is designed around the same VT/RT and NDT Level II workflows used under ASNT SNT-TC-1A. It's built to fit existing QA/QC and HSE procedures, not replace them.

Do we need to replace our existing systems?

No. VetriX•IQ is designed to run alongside your current documentation process, so teams can migrate module by module — starting with whichever workflow is causing the most pain today.

Is our data secure and access-controlled?

Yes. VetriX•IQ uses secure cloud authentication with role-based permissions, so inspectors, supervisors and QA/QC managers only see what's relevant to their role.

Can field teams use this without a desktop?

Yes. The platform is cloud-based and responsive, so it works from a laptop in the office or a phone or tablet in the field — no separate mobile app to install.

Who is VetriX•IQ built for?

QA/QC teams, NDT inspectors, HSE departments and EPC contractors in oil & gas and other industrial sectors that run weld, corrosion or personnel qualification programs.

What happens during a demo?

A walkthrough of VetriX•IQ tailored to a workflow that looks like yours — weld inspection, corrosion monitoring or personnel qualification tracking — plus a direct conversation about your team's specific documentation gaps.

What does VetriX•IQ cost?

There's no public price list yet — VetriX•IQ is in active development, and pricing is scoped to your team's size and the modules you need. We'll cover it directly on the demo call.
