01 / About

Two disciplines, one standard: get it right the first time

I started inspecting welds on Iraq's southern oilfields while finishing a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering — spending my days verifying pipelines and OCTG against international standards, and my evenings writing code. Ten years later those two tracks have merged: I'm still the inspector who reads radiographic film and signs off Permit-to-Work packages, and I'm also the developer who builds the software those same field teams actually need.

That combination is the reason VetriX•IQ exists. Most inspection software is built by people who have never stood next to a weld. Mine is built from years of doing the job it digitizes — which is why it models the workflow correctly the first time.
